Privacy policy

rclone-esos requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, which covers files that this app itself creates or that the user explicitly opens with it.

All data moves directly between the owner's computers and the owner's Google Drive. Nothing is sent to, stored on, or shared with any other server or third party, and no analytics or tracking is used.

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party connections.
